Executive Snapshot · AI Governance
Waktu baca 5–7 menit

Your staff already use AI. Who’s checking what they share?

Governed AI is easier to scale, easier to trust, and easier to defend when something goes wrong.

A sales manager pastes 200 customer names and phone numbers into a free AI tool to draft follow-up messages. It saves her an hour. No one approved the tool. No one knows where the data went. Under UU PDP, the company already has to answer for it.
68%
of organizations hit by a data breach had no rules for how AI should be used. A year earlier, it was 63%.
Sumber: IBM, Cost of a Data Breach Report 2026
01 · Results

Companies with CEO oversight see more value.

CEO oversight of AI governance is among the factors most linked to higher bottom-line impact from gen AI (McKinsey 2025).

02 · Speed

Clear rules mean faster approvals.

Simple AI uses get a quick yes. Risky ones get the attention they need.

03 · Trust

Proof beats promises.

A documented answer wins the deal. ISO/IEC 42001 gives you a standard to show it.

04 · Cost

Unapproved AI makes breaches costlier.

US$5.39M on average vs. US$4.99M overall (about Rp 98 miliar vs. Rp 91 miliar).

Exhibit A

43% of incidents involved unapproved AI

More than double the 20% a year earlier.

Di Indonesia

Hanya 16% perusahaan punya tim tata kelola AI

Di sisanya tanggung jawab dibagi antar tim, sering berarti tidak ada yang memegang (AWS & Strand Partners 2026).

What to do next: beri staf alat AI yang disetujui sebelum memblokir yang gratis. Lalu minta finance dan procurement menandai langganan AI di luar jalur resmi.
Ungoverned AI looks cheaper — until the incident.

Employees adopt AI faster than the organization decides how AI should be used. Governance then has to catch up with habits rather than shape them.
01 · Q01–02

Accountability

A named owner for every AI decision.

02 · Q03–04

Visibility

Knowing where AI is used and what it touches.

03 · Q05–06

Data Protection

Lawful, assessed use of personal data.

04 · Q07

Security

Access controlled in both directions.

05 · Q08

Human Oversight

People who know when to intervene.

06 · Q09–10

Assurance

Evidence, response, and review on demand.

Governance is what lets an organization say yes to AI with confidence.

Executive Brief · Oktober 2026
PP 33/2026 berlaku 16 Januari 2027

Before AI Touches Your Data

10 Governance Questions Every Leadership Team Should Answer.

Tiga keputusan untuk rapat pimpinan berikutnya

1 · Name the owner.

Tunjuk satu eksekutif yang bertanggung jawab atas risiko AI.

2 · See what is in use.

Buat inventaris alat AI, termasuk yang tidak disetujui.

3 · Set the approval rule.

Tentukan AI mana yang cepat disetujui dan mana yang perlu asesmen dampak.

7kategori berisiko tinggi pemicu DPIA
3×24jam notifikasi kegagalan data
2%batas denda administratif dari pendapatan tahunan
10×pengali denda pidana korporasi
  1. Who is accountable for AI decisions in our organization?
  2. Do our Directors and Commissioners see AI risk the way they see financial risk?
  3. Do we know where AI is already being used — including tools no one approved?
  4. Do we know what data each AI system touches — and where it goes?
  5. Do we have a lawful basis for every use of personal data in AI?
  6. Have we assessed high-risk AI processing before it begins?
  7. Who can access our AI systems — and what can those systems access?
  8. Where must a human remain in the decision — and do our people know how to intervene?
  9. If an AI-related data incident happened tonight, could we respond in time?
  10. Could we prove our AI governance to a regulator, auditor, or customer tomorrow?
If a regulator, a customer, and your board asked the same question about AI tomorrow — would they hear the same answer?
